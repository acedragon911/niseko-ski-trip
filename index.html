✅ Gate rules (Niseko Rules)闸门规则（二世谷规则）

  • Leave the resort only through an open gate. Never duck a rope. Closed gate = no entry.
  • Wear a helmet and carry a beacon, probe and shovel, and know how to use them.
  • Never alone: go in pairs at least, and keep each other in sight (tree wells).
  • Check the daily Niseko avalanche report and the sign at the gate.
  • Rescue outside the boundary is charged, and your travel insurance must cover off-piste.
  • Ask the rental shop for wider powder skis. They make deep snow much easier.
  • 只能从开放的闸门离开雪场，绝不能钻绳。闸门关闭就不能进入。
  • 戴头盔，携带信标、探杆和雪铲，并且会用。
  • 不要单独行动：至少两人一起，互相保持在视线内（小心树井）。
  • 查看每日二世谷雪崩信息和闸门处的告示。
  • 边界外的救援要收费，旅游保险必须包含雪道外滑雪。
  • 向租赁店要宽板粉雪板，在深雪里会轻松很多。

🧤 Cold & what to pack寒冷与装备

  • Mid-January: about −9 to −2°C in the village, colder up top with wind chill below −20°C on bad days. ~30 snow days a month, so expect grey flat light more than bluebird. Sunrise ~7:00, sunset ~16:30.
  • Pack: a low-light goggle lens (yellow, rose or amber) for flat light; a balaclava or neck gaiter; mittens over thin liners; base + mid + shell layers. Buy kairo hand/toe warmers at any konbini. Keep phones in an inner pocket with a power bank. Sunscreen for the odd clear day.
  • Slope rules: don't save lift-line spots with gear. Smoke only in marked areas. Drones need resort permission. Breaking the Niseko Rules can cost your lift pass, and rescue starts at ¥100,000.
  • 一月中旬：村里约 −9 到 −2°C，山顶更冷，大风天体感低于 −20°C。一个月约30天下雪，多是灰白平光，晴天少。日出约7:00，日落约16:30。
  • 带上：弱光雪镜片（黄/玫瑰/琥珀色）应对平光；面罩或脖套；连指手套配薄内手套；排汗层 + 中间层 + 外壳。便利店买暖宝宝（手/脚）。手机放内袋并带充电宝。偶尔晴天要防晒。
  • 雪场规矩：不能用装备占排队位置；只在指定区域吸烟；无人机需雪场许可；违反二世谷规则会被没收缆车票，救援 ¥100,000 起。

⛷️ On the mountain在雪场

  • Someone hurt on the slope: stay with them, cross a pair of skis upright above them so others see you, and tell the nearest lift attendant or ski patrol. Off the slope or no staff around: call 119.
  • Never ski alone in the trees. Tree wells (deep holes of loose snow around trees) are a real danger in Niseko powder. Keep your buddy in sight.
  • Niseko Rules: leave the resort boundary only through the open gates. Never duck under ropes. Rescue outside the boundary is charged. Check the daily Niseko avalanche report.
  • If the group gets split up: meet at Hanazono 308 at the base, or in the Park Hyatt lobby.
  • Keep phones charged (the cold drains batteries fast). Share live location in the group chat on ski days.
  • 有人在雪道上受伤：留在伤者身边，把一副雪板交叉竖插在伤者上方提醒他人，并通知最近的缆车工作人员或雪场巡逻。不在雪道上或附近没有工作人员：拨打119。
  • 不要独自滑树林。二世谷粉雪里的"树井"（树周围的深层松雪坑）很危险。始终保持同伴在视线内。
  • 二世谷规则：只能从开放的闸门离开雪场边界，绝不能钻绳。边界外的救援要收费。请查看每日二世谷雪崩信息。
  • 如果走散：在山脚的花园308或柏悦酒店大堂集合。
  • 手机保持电量充足（低温耗电很快）。滑雪日在群里共享实时位置。
