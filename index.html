Breakfast at the hotel, lunch on the slopes, dinner below (bookings still being checked).

  • Return rental gear by 10 am the next day (free late return at DIAMOND X).
Sun Jan 17Check out
Breakfast, return gear, check out, transfer to CTS.
🎆 Hanazono "Fireworks Saturday" 2026–27: Sat Jan 16, 2027
Last winter, Hanazono set off fireworks every Saturday of the night-skiing season (Dec 13, 2025 – Mar 21, 2026). The 2026–27 dates and start time are not posted yet, so check the Hanazono events page closer to the trip. The show is short (about 5 minutes), so book dinner either before it or right after it. It is visible from the hotel and the base area. The show can be postponed if the weather is bad.

2026–27 peak prices (Dec 24 – Feb 28), in yen. Online is cheaper than buying at the counter. Source: Hanazono official lift tickets.

Best for this trip

Niseko United All Mountain

Covers all 4 resorts: Hanazono, Grand Hirafu, Niseko Village and Annupuri. Worth it if the group of 5 wants to explore on Jan 14–16.

Cheaper

Hanazono + Hirafu

About 60% of Niseko United's terrain. Hanazono and Hirafu connect on the mountain. It also has a night pass and a PM 4-hour ticket.

Trail map

Niseko United 2026–27: Hanazono (far right/east), Grand Hirafu, Niseko Village, Annupuri.

  • Hanazono: the hotel's home slopes. Wide groomers, tree runs and the Hanazono 308 base.
  • To Hirafu: take the lifts up and cross over the top. Hanazono and Hirafu connect on the mountain.
  • Village / Annupuri: All Mountain pass only. Cross via the summit area when gates are open, or take the Niseko United shuttle bus.
  • Check lift and gate status every morning. Wind often closes the upper lifts in January.

  • Teppan: French-inspired teppanyaki (closed Mon & Tue)
  • Olivio: Italian, good for families and groups
  • China Kitchen: Cantonese and Sichuan
